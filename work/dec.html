Problem Statement

Traditional banner ads have become too familiar — and often ignored — by mobile app users. As a result, their conversion rates are declining.

Creating and scaling banners, especially dynamic or personalized ones, is also resource-intensive.

Operational costs were high, but efficiency remained low due to manual workflows.

It was difficult to scale or personalize content across different user scenarios, contexts, and data points — making the experience feel generic and resource-heavy to maintain.

Solution

To address this, we introduced a new feature: Dynamic Engagement Content (DEC) – a smarter, automated solution, more adaptive way to connect with users in real time.

01

How it works?

Dynamic Engagement Content (DEC) is a smart feature created to reduce manual work and improve the user experience.

  • For the operations team, DEC helps reduce more than 75% of workload by using AI to tag data, build preset campaigns, and select the right content. It can:
    • Automatically choose products based on user habits
    • Create communication flows for flash sales, ticketing, food delivery
    • Send smart reminders: expiring vouchers, upgrade tips, exclusive benefits, and more
  • For users, DEC shows personalized and fresh content every time they open the app — helping them discover the most relevant and updateders.

Here’s how DEC helps reduce operational effort compared to traditional banner-based campaigns.
